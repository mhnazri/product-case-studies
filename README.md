# Product Case Studies

**Selected case studies on translating domain knowledge into privacy-conscious digital products.**

This repository documents the product thinking, scope decisions and implementation lessons behind selected **Peekspective** products built by Mohd Nazri Yaakub.

The objective is to show how complex domain problems become focused, explainable and usable software—not to publish the products' private source code.

## Case Studies

| Product | Focus | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [PeekLens Studio](products/peeklens-studio.md) | Cloud-based EDI/EDIFACT validation and tooling | Live |
| [PeekLens Lite](products/peeklens-lite.md) | Offline-first UN/EDIFACT learning and syntax inspection | Available on the App Store · Android Closed Testing |
| [PeekGarage Lite](products/peekgarage-lite.md) | Privacy-first vehicle lifecycle management | In development · Internal TestFlight testing |

## What These Case Studies Demonstrate

- Translating specialist domain knowledge into product requirements
- Setting clear boundaries around validation, privacy and trust
- Making complex information visible and explainable
- Decision-first, test-conscious solo product development
- Building coherent products across mobile and web

## Publication Boundary

The case studies contain original descriptions, safe architectural decisions and sanitized examples. They do not expose private source code, proprietary Message Implementation Guides, production payloads, customer or employer data, credentials, or sensitive infrastructure details.
